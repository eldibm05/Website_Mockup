Why Membership

People join movements they believe in — not organizations they've merely heard of. Membership is how you go from attending MAC Calgary to building it: shared identity, real brotherhood and sisterhood, and a seat in shaping how our community lives its faith here, in the West.

The Pathway

Five stages, one direction: forward.

Membership isn't a single application — it's a relationship that grows. Here's what each stage actually looks like and roughly how long it takes.

01

Community Contact

You show up — a lecture, an iftar, a friend's invite. No commitment, just presence.

Day one
02

Open Halaqah Attendee

You start coming to our open halaqat regularly. No sign-up required — just walk in.

Ongoing
03

Adjunct Stage

A naqeeb personally reaches out. This is where real relationship and mentorship begin.

~2–3 months
04

Membership Invitation

Once you're ready, you're formally invited to apply — no guesswork about when or how.

~2–4 weeks
05

Activated Member

You're in — with a welcome, a point of contact, and a clear next step into committees.

Common Questions

What people actually ask us.

Start by walking into an open halaqah — no sign-up needed. From there, a naqeeb will reach out personally during the Adjunct Stage to build a relationship before any formal invitation happens. See the full pathway above for what to expect at each point.
Activated members have a voice in chapter direction, access to committee roles, and a standing point of contact — alongside the responsibility to participate actively, not just hold a title. A full breakdown is shared at the point of invitation.
Right away. Open halaqat, MAC Give community service, and youth events are open from day one — membership formalizes involvement, it doesn't gate it.
It varies by person, but most people move from their first open halaqah to a membership invitation within a few months of consistent involvement — there's no fixed clock, just consistent next steps.
