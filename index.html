Membership

Belong to something that actually matters.

Membership at MAC Calgary isn't a form you fill out. It's what happens when showing up turns into belonging, and belonging turns into building something together. Here's what that looks like, and where to start.

Why Membership

Membership is how you go from attending MAC Calgary to building it: shared identity, real brotherhood and sisterhood, and a seat in shaping how our community lives its faith here in the West.

The Pathway

Three stages, one direction: forward.

Membership isn't a single application. It's a relationship that grows over time. Here's what each stage looks like.

01

Open Halaqah

You start coming to our open halaqat regularly. Low commitment, just walk in and learn something.

Find an open halaqah
02

Adjunct

Apply to join an adjunct halaqah, or get approached by a halaqah mentor. Commitment and responsibilities increase as you build real relationships and grow in your Islam and within the organization.

About 2 years
03

Membership

Once you finish the adjunct stage, you'll receive a formal invitation to become a member and take on more responsibility in the work of the chapter.

Common Questions

Questions we get asked a lot.

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and we'll get back to you.

Start by coming to one of our open halaqat. When you're ready for more, you can apply for an adjunct halaqah, or a halaqah mentor may reach out to you. After about two years in the adjunct stage, you'll be invited to become a member. See the pathway for more on each stage.

Members commit to attending their halaqah regularly and being involved in at least one program within the chapter. It's about showing up consistently, not just holding a title.

You get a more active voice in chapter affairs, a real brotherhood and sisterhood to lean on, and a part in a collective working toward meaningful change in our community.

Right away. Our open halaqat are open to everyone, and you can volunteer at chapter events and programs from day one. You don't need to be a member to start contributing.

The adjunct stage takes about two years. That time is for building relationships, growing in your Islam, and taking on more responsibility, so that by the time you're invited to become a member, you're ready for it.
